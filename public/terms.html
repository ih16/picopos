Terms of service

picopos lets you manage inventory and orders in a Google spreadsheet you own or have permission to edit. Each staff member connects with their own Google account. The workbook owner manages access through Google Drive sharing settings.

Edits made offline are stored in your browser until they sync. Check pending changes before disconnecting or clearing site data. Review stock and order records when multiple people edit at the same time.
