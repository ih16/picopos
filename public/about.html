picopos

Manage a shop’s products, inventory, customers, and orders in a Google spreadsheet you own. Use the dashboard to review sales and stock, record purchases and returns, and work offline until your changes can sync.

How it uses Google Sheets

Choose or create a spreadsheet through Google. picopos reads and writes that workbook directly from your browser. Each staff member connects using their own Google account, and you control workbook access in Google Drive. You can also explore the demo with sample records before connecting.
