picopos

Manage a shop’s products, inventory, customers, and orders in a Google spreadsheet you own. Use the dashboard to review sales and stock, record purchases and returns, and work offline until your changes can sync.

What you can do in picopos

Record purchase lots when stock arrives, reserve items for an order, and record returns against that order. The dashboard uses those records to show sales and available stock. Product photos are optional catalog thumbnails.

Why picopos asks for Google access

Google sign-in is optional. To save real shop records, choose a spreadsheet in Google Picker or create one in picopos. The Google Drive permission applies to files you authorize through the app. picopos reads and writes that workbook directly from your browser. Each staff member connects using their own Google account, and you control workbook access in Google Drive. You can also explore the demo with sample records before connecting.
