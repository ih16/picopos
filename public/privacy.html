picopos privacy policy

Updated November 19, 2026

Google data picopos accesses

When you connect Google Sheets, picopos requests access to Google Drive files you select in the picker or create through the app. It reads and updates your spreadsheet to manage products, orders, customer details, and inventory. This includes the workbook identifier and title, product names and photos, prices, stock movements, order history, and customer contact and delivery details entered by your staff. The demo uses sample records and requires no Google authorization.

Where data goes

Your browser exchanges authorization information with Google and sends workbook edits directly to Google Sheets. The static site is hosted on Vercel, which receives ordinary web requests when you load the app. picopos has no separate server that receives your workbook contents.

How Google data is used and shared

picopos uses the selected spreadsheet to display your shop records, calculate stock and sales, and synchronize changes you make. Staff with access to the shared workbook can see the records in it. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI models. Google access tokens authorize requests from your browser and expire. Revoking access stops future authorized requests.

How stored data is protected

Requests to Google use HTTPS. picopos encrypts saved workbook snapshots, queued edits, and remembered access tokens with browser-held keys. A person with access to your unlocked browser profile may still open your offline workbook. Use a trusted device for shop records.

Retention, disconnection, and deletion

Your browser keeps a local workbook copy, pending edits, and a remembered Google session. Local records can remain after you close the tab or your Google authorization expires. Sync pending edits before disconnecting. Disconnect removes the current local workbook and remembered session. To remove other local copies, clear site data for this origin after syncing. The spreadsheet stays in Google Drive, where you control sharing and deletion.

You can revoke picopos access in your Google Account connections. For privacy questions or help removing data, email the picopos support contact. To change or delete records in a shared shop workbook, contact its owner.
