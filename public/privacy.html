picopos

Privacy policy

When you connect Google Sheets, picopos requests access to Google Drive files you select in the picker or create through the app. It reads and updates your spreadsheet to manage products, orders, customer details, and inventory. Google enforces the sharing permissions you set for each workbook.

Where data goes

Your browser exchanges authorization information with Google and sends workbook edits directly to Google Sheets. The static site is hosted on Vercel, which receives ordinary web requests when you load the app. picopos has no separate server that receives your workbook contents.

How Google data is used and shared

picopos uses the selected spreadsheet to display your shop records, calculate stock and sales, and synchronize changes you make. Staff with access to the shared workbook can see the records in it. The app does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to AI models. Google access tokens authorize requests from your browser and expire. Revoking access stops future authorized requests.

Offline storage and removal

Your browser keeps a local workbook copy, pending edits, and a remembered Google session. Sync pending edits before disconnecting. Disconnect removes the current local workbook and remembered session. To remove other local copies, clear site data for this origin after syncing. The spreadsheet stays in Google Drive, where you control sharing and deletion.

You can revoke picopos access in your Google Account connections. For a privacy question, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
